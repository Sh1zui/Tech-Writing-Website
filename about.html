About this project

This website is my output for the ENG 2 Final Examination: Making a Website at City College of Calapan. It organizes seven technical writing topics into one easy-to-navigate study guide:

  1. Technical Writing: Definition and Purpose
  2. ABCs of Technical Writing
  3. Outlining the Subject
  4. Types of Technical Paper
  5. The Basic English Structure
  6. Parts of a Letter
  7. Graphs and Charts

Every lecture has explanations, examples, visuals and a short self-check quiz. Start reading the lectures or send me a message.

Skills this course builds

Clarity

Choosing precise words and writing sentences that a reader cannot misread.

Organization

Outlining a subject so each idea sits in its logical place.

Presentation

Formatting letters and reports and presenting data with graphic aids.
