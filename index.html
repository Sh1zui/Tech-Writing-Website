Purpose of this website

Technical writing turns specialized knowledge into something readers can use.

This site gathers the key ideas from my ENG 2 lessons in one organized place, with examples, visuals and self-check questions, so that any student can review them quickly. Technical writing has three main purposes:

To informGive readers the facts they need, such as in a memo or manual.
To persuade and influenceConvince readers to approve a plan, as in a proposal.
To analyze eventsExplain what happened and why, as in an incident report.
